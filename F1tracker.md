Ops Summary
We arrived on site with around 23,000 pre-printed tags. 12 associates were assigned to help and two departed the first day after putting in little to no effort. Quite a few knew very little english so the teaching of the SOP became a telephone effort from the two associates that can translate for us. This also meant we were very limited in who we could staff in the FOH to support customer questions and problems.
We also arrived on site blind with no real understanding of the expected effort and setup for stocking/receiving products which meant we had more waste than originally estimated in terms of products on the list we’ve never stocked ( ~3,000 tags) not including the extra we had to print backfill the space on shelves.

CXQO:  TTs were being cut for RFID tags being read that do not belong to Amazon. We need to reach out to CS and see what their SOP is. As an org we need to acknowledge that there are companies out there with RFID embedded products used for their own logistics and it will eventually be seen in our gates.
