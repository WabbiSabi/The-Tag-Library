📡 RIOT RFID — Temp Employee Quick Guide

Everything you need to scan inventory on your first day

⚡ First — Log Into Your Site

Every store location has its own unique QR code. You must scan your site's QR code and log in before you can scan any products. Your manager or team lead will provide this.

1

Get your site's QR code from your manager. This is generated from the RIOT portal under Settings → App Provisioning and is specific to your store.

2

Open the RIOT app on your scanner device. Go to Settings → Quick Start and scan the QR code.

3

Enter your Tenant ID when prompted. Your manager will provide this. (You can also find it by clicking your username in the top-right of the RIOT portal.)

4

Close and relaunch the app. You're now logged into your site and ready to scan.

⚠️ Each site has a different QR code. If you move to a different store location, you'll need to scan that site's QR code before you can begin scanning products there.
A Before You Start — Check Your Equipment
1

Check scanner charge. Look at the LED light on the side of your scanner:

Light ColorMeaning
GreenFully charged — good to go!
YellowCurrently charging
RedLow charge — plug in before scanning
No lightDead battery — charge immediately
2

Check Wi-Fi connection. Open the RIOT app. Look at the top-right corner — you'll see two small icons. Both should be green. If either is red or missing, you don't have a connection.

3

Connect the RFID reader to the Android device. The signal icon in the top-right will turn green when connected. If it's red, the reader isn't connected — make sure it's charged and paired.

B Scanning Inventory
1

Open "Inventory Taking" from the app menu (tap the ☰ hamburger icon top-left).

2

Press the green Play ▶ button (bottom-left) to start scanning. Walk through your assigned area steadily.

💡 Tip: The scanner beeps as it reads tags. When the beeping slows down, most items in that area have been captured — move to the next section.
3

Fan through tightly packed items with your free hand as you scan.

⚠️ Metal blocks RFID! The scanner reads through cardboard, plastic, and thin walls — but metal surfaces block tags. Items fully behind metal won't scan.
C Event Check In / Check Out

Event Check In and Event Check Out let you add or remove scanned items from a site's total inventory count.

▶

Event Check In — Use this to add scanned EPCs to the site's total inventory. When new product arrives or is moved into your location, scan it using Event Check In so it's counted as part of your site.

◀

Event Check Out — Use this to remove scanned EPCs from the site's total inventory. When product leaves your location (sold, transferred, returned to vendor, etc.), scan it using Event Check Out so it's no longer counted at your site.

1

Open the app menu (tap ☰ top-left) and select either Event Check In or Event Check Out.

2

Scan the items the same way you would for a normal inventory scan — press the Play ▶ button and walk through the area.

3

Submit your scan when finished. The EPCs will be added to (Check In) or removed from (Check Out) your site's inventory total.

💡 Tip: Think of it like a front door — Check In = product walking in, Check Out = product walking out. Use the right one so your site's inventory count stays accurate.
⚠️ Double-check which mode you're in! Using Check In when you meant Check Out (or vice versa) will throw off your site's inventory totals. Confirm the screen header before you start scanning.
D Submitting Your Scan
1

Tap the three dots ⋮ in the top-right corner of the Inventory Taking screen.

2

Select "Submit Hand Scan" from the dropdown menu.

3

Name your scan. Keep the default date. Do NOT change it unless your manager says otherwise.

⚠️ Important: If multiple people are scanning at the same time, all scans with the EXACT same name will automatically combine into one report. Keep the default date so everything merges correctly.
E Troubleshooting — Quick Fixes
?

Need to restart your scan?

Tap ⋮ (three dots) → select "Reset Stock Counters" to clear and start over.

?

App frozen or acting up?

Close the app: tap the square button (bottom-right of device) → swipe away or "X" the RIOT app. Reopen it — you'll be asked if you want to reload your last scan. Your progress is safe.

?

Scanner not connecting?

Check the side light is on (charged). Turn scanner off and back on, then reopen the RIOT app.

✓ Pre-Scan Checklist
  • Scanner is charged (green light on side)
  • RIOT app is open on the Android device
  • Wi-Fi is connected (green icon, top-right)
  • RFID reader is connected (green signal icon, top-right)
  • Correct store / location is showing
  • I know my assigned scan area
  • I'll keep the default date name when submitting
