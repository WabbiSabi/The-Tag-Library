Q: How do I power on the Zebra printer?

A: **Plug in the power supply** and verify the screen displays "Idle." Press and **hold the ⏻ power button** until the indicator lights flash. Once the **Status LED turns solid green**, the printer is ready.

Q: How do I install the thermal transfer ribbon?

A: Load the ribbon onto the **bottom dispenser** feeding **under**. Wrap the loose end around the empty reel (tape if needed), pull over the print edge, and secure into the **top dispenser**. Rotate the **top-left knob** to tighten. Test by pressing a sticker onto the ribbon — ink should transfer.

Q: How do I pair the printer with a TC handheld device?

A: **1.** Verify **Bluetooth is enabled** (blue icon) on the TC device. **2.** Open the **IhmCandyCane** app. **3.** Find the printer's **Bluetooth MAC Address** under Printer Info on the printer screen. **4.** Tap the **☰ menu** › **Store Tools** › **Scan, Validate, & Print**. **5.** Enter the MAC address (use colons **:** as shown) and tap **Pair**. A "Pairing complete" message confirms success.

Q: What do the status lights mean?

A: The printer has 5 indicator LEDs:

- **Status** — Solid green = ready. Solid red = error (media out, cover open). Amber = printhead issue.
- **Pause** — Amber = printer paused. Blinking red = overheating.
- **Data** — Blinking green = data transferring. Solid green = data pending.
- **Supplies** — Solid red = media out. Blinking red = ribbon out.
- **Network** — Green = connected. Red = fault. Blinking = associating.

Q: What do the printer buttons do?

A: **⏻ Power** — Hold to turn on/off. Tap to enter sleep mode. **⏸ Pause** — Pauses/resumes printing. **▶ Feed** — Advances one label (hold to keep advancing). **✖ Cancel** — Only works while paused. Press once to cancel next label, hold 2 seconds to cancel all jobs.
