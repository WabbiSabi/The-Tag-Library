Tag Application Process

The AD Midas Flag-Tags have been the easiest tag to apply to merchandise compared to the rest seen in the labs (AD146, AD262, AD457, M8, and U9) in either sticker or HT form.
Operationally this is because the antenna portion (roughly 50%) is the only part with adhesive as opposed to the “band-aid” like separation needed for the M8 tag backing.
Note: Experience level is dictated by how familiar the applier with the concept of attaching a sticker or HT to merchandise.

Test conclusion:

 Six-pack tagging averages 26 seconds total (4 seconds per can) with pre-opened cases, while intact cases take 32 seconds (5 seconds per can), with most of the additional time spent carefully opening the packaging rather than tagging.
