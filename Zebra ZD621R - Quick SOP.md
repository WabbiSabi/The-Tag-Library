Q: How do I install tags in the Zebra printer?

A: Place the tag roll with the **HT hole on the left side**. Feed the tags "toilet paper roll" style — **over, not under**.

Q: How do I place the ink roll?

A: Place the ink roll on the **bottom dispenser** (yellow knobs), "toilet paper" style **under**. Wrap the loose end around an empty cardboard reel, pull up and over the printing edge, then place the wrapped reel into the **top dispenser**.

Q: How do I test if the ink ribbon is on the correct side?

A: Place a piece of tape or spare sticker on the ink ribbon, then gently peel it away. If installed correctly, **the ink should transfer to the tape/sticker**.

A: The mount comes in **6 pieces**. Assemble it, place the tag roll ("toilet paper side" over), position it **behind the printer**, and feed the tags through the **slot in the back**.

Q: How do I calibrate the printer?

A: **Manual Calibration:** Turn on power, then press and hold **⏸ + ✖ for 2 seconds**. Then attempt to print a tag in TCDevice. Press ⏸ for each print job.

**RFID Calibration:** Wizard > RFID > Start RFID > select country > Start Calibration.

**RFID Check:** Menu > RFID Button > RFID > select **RFID Calibrate** to auto-adjust power levels for the installed tags.
