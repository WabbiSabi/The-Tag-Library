Scan, Validate, & Print — Quick SOP

App: IhmCandyCane  |  Device: Zebra TC Handheld  |  Printer: Zebra ZD621R

1 Enable Bluetooth

  1. Tap the square icon next to the ⚙ symbol
  2. Verify Bluetooth is enabled (icon should be blue)
  3. Exit the menu

2 Open IhmCandyCane App

  1. Find and open the IhmCandyCane app on the home screen

3 Find Printer MAC Address

  1. On the Zebra printer, navigate to Printer Info on the display screen
  2. Note down the Bluetooth MAC Address — you'll need it for pairing

4 Open Scan, Validate, & Print

  1. Tap the ☰ menu in the top-left corner
  2. Under Just Walk Out › Store Tools, scroll down
  3. Select Scan, Validate & Print

5 Pair Printer

  1. The Scan, Validate & Print screen will show "Disconnected" (orange badge)
  2. A bottom panel will prompt: Enter Mac Address of Printer
  3. Type the MAC address from the printer using colons (e.g. 00:22:33:CC:DD)
  4. Tap the Pair button
  5. Wait for "Pairing with printer" progress bar to complete
  6. Status badge will change from "Disconnected" to "Connected"
⚠️ If pairing fails, tap the Recent Apps icon in the upper right, confirm the Bluetooth icon is blue, then try again.

6 Scan & Print Tags

  1. Press the side button on the handheld to scan the product barcode
  2. Adjust the quantity using the + / − buttons
  3. Verify the product on screen matches what you scanned
  4. Tap Print
  5. Collect all printed tags from the printer

6a Image Not Found? Use PICT

⚠️ If the product has no image on file, the TC device will automatically redirect you to Product Image Capture (PICT). You must upload images before you can print a tag.
  1. The device will open Product Image Capture (PICT) automatically
  2. Take a clear photo of the front of the item
  3. Take a clear photo of the back of the item
  4. Review both images — use Retake if either photo is unclear
  5. Tap Upload to submit the images
  6. Once uploaded, you'll be returned to the print screen to continue
💡 You will need to re-photograph the same product for different sizes. Each size requires its own set of images.

6b "Not Printable" — Failed Validations

🚫 If the item isn't ready to be printed, you'll see a "Not Printable" screen with validation details. Follow the on-screen guidance to resolve.
  1. If you see "Not Printable — Barcode Failed Validations", check the two validation rows:
ValidationStatusWhat to Do
Product validation checks ✓ Passed (green) No action needed
Image validation checks ⚠ Failed (red) Tap the › arrow to open PICT and upload front + back photos (see Step 6a)
Product validation checks ⚠ Failed (red) Product is missing from catalog — Location Manager must update it in the Merchant Portal
💡 The info banner at the top says: "To resolve the error, click on the arrow in the red box." Tap the › next to the failed check to begin resolving it.
