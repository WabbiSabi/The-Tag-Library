Cosmic- JAX, FL: 1st Game Notes

Cannot stress enough how timing is key for touring, more so than pop-ups due to the extra moving parts.



* Launch Date: 03/19 
* Location: Vystar Arena Jacksonville
* Block Party Start: 15:00
* Game Start: 19:00

1. Outside Space - Aka Block Party

[1A] Entrance of the Block Party and ballpark. Why is it laid out like this?

[1B] Other side of the “block party”. Only one way in/out.
[1C] ~30 associates arrived on site at around 14:00 for training. 12 of those associates were being trained on POS by Gabe while the rest just stood around waiting for assignments.

[1D] The outside “store” is four tents long.

[1E] There was a lot of room next to the tents as the riot fence set up a large rectangle and Cosmic prioritized tents directly facing the entrance to ball-park. This caused the queue to block the entrance as noted in photo[1C].

[1F] Block Party zigzagging in front of tent causing congestion to entrance of the park.



[1G] There are only two POSs per table/tent but four associates staffed. The idea is there are two “cashiers” and two runners. However, the space is so small there is no need for that many runners, if at all as the speed of the sale is hinged on the POS speed.

[1H] There is only one or two “closet” spaces for storage of merchandised so the supplies for tents located inside the stadiums will be prioritized there and re-stock for the outdoors tent are pulled directly from trailers.


2. Inside the Stadium

[2A] VIP Lounge, per Christopher there is only the same amount of people every time, ~120
[2B] Inside the stadium there are three more cluster of tents besides VIP room, totaling around 9 tents inside the stadium. 
[2C] Per Christopher, there wouldn’ve been any room for JWO gates as the photo above is the biggest space available and there are ADA requirements for spacing between tent and seating.

[2D] Per Christopher, this is pretty standard set up at sporting venues. The biggest seller/opportunity will be the tents outside as this portion opens hours before game begin and customers will need to pass those merch tents to go inside.

[2H] Lights Out: Prior to lights out, there is a 30minute break where players change outfits and light-up bubble wands in carts are sold [2G]

[2E] Selection inside venue is the same as outside: Primarily Chili Peppers gear.
The two other tents are the “competing” teams merch which there are less selection of.

[2F] Items are slowly being moved to be distributed to the inside tents as the Block Party needs to be shut down before game starts.


[2G] Rolling carts full of light-up bubble wands. These start rolling around the venue ~1hr before “lights out” for customers to get the opportunity to purchase.




* Some Ops Notes at this point:
    * Very limited access to elevators/freight elevators. So merch ends up having to go up several floors all the way across the field from where they were supposed to go - mix up risk
    * All items coming from the Block Party are integrated into the merchandise within venue for last sale opportunities before Lights Out - More mixing risk
    * Average basket per family of 2: ~6-8 items

    * Cosmic did not end up tagging the markers ($3 in POS)

3. Behind the Scenes

[3A] Three trailers arrived on site Tuesday night (03/17) where indoor-tent merchandise were moved into the basement. This site was noted to be special as there was a room with table/chairs for team to set up printers and POS systems for charging- dont expect this space for other venues.

[3B] While game is happening, associates are tasked with counting products with the goal of filling up these chests for the next games/stop.


[3C] Avg “BOH” space if there were no rooms available for stocking/printing.




Issue(s) Reported:

1. Ink ribbon gets pulled out as tag prints: SOLVED

Customer reported the ink ribbon constantly gets pulled out as tag prints. Upon opening the printer, I noted the ink roll was not “latched” into the divet so the tape spun on the spindel- causing the ribbon being pulled as the tags were being pushed out.


1. 2 of 4 Printers with constant connectivity Issues: WIP (Reached out to RIOT 03/20)

I recalibrated everything and it all lined up with the tag serration points. Customer reported the printers kept going offline and they had to waste time and effort on re-syncing the laptop and printer from the Insight dashboard. Emailed Jace
