Zebra ZD621R — Quick Setup

Thermal Transfer Ribbon: 4.33″ × 984′

1 Power On

  1. Plug in the power supply
  2. Hold ⏻ power button until lights flash
  3. Screen will appear

2 Load Ribbon

  1. Pull yellow latches on each side to open
  2. Ribbon roll on bottom — feed ribbon under the roll
  3. Pull over print edge → wrap onto top spool → tighten yellow knob
🧪 Ink test: press a sticker onto the ribbon — ink should transfer. If not, flip the roll.

3 Load RFID Tags

  1. Place roll in holder — HT holes on the LEFT
  2. Feed tags through the back slot
  3. Thread through yellow guides and out the front
  4. Push guides snug → close lid until it clicks
⚠️ Make sure the sensor sits in the middle of the printer — not off to one side.

4 Calibrate

  1. Close the printer lid
  2. Hold ⏸ Pause + ✖ Cancel for 2 seconds
  3. Wait for printer to return to "Idle"
⚠️ Do this every time you load new ribbon or tags, or if you see "VOID" prints.

5 Pair with Handheld

  1. Find the printer's Bluetooth MAC address (Printer Info screen)
  2. On TC device: IhmCandyCane › ☰ › Store Tools › Scan, Validate, & Print
  3. Enter MAC address with colons (example AC:3F:A4:12:5B:9E) → tap Pair
  4. Wait for "Pairing complete ✔"
