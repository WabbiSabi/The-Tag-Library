Closing Summary:

The site deployment for this event revealed significant operational challenges stemming from inadequate pre-event coordination and communication gaps due to the customer's concurrent launch activities. Limited customer engagement prior to the event left the tagging ops aspect with minimal guidance, particularly regarding tagging protocols and inventory management requirements.
Based on the customer's initial estimate of 7,000 tags, the deployment team shipped RFID tag to the site before 01/19 with accompanying SOPs. However, the decision to maintain minimal Amazonian presence on-site until launch day, created downstream impacts when the JWO team discovered that all beverage inventory had been pre-tagged incorrectly. Associates had placed tags at the top of cans rather than in optimal positions, resulting in elevated IPK/EPK rates. With thousands of misplaced tags and limited staffing, the JWO team was limited in corrective options.

Julie, the tent manager, became the primary point of contact for ongoing operational needs throughout the entire event as PoTP contact was coordinating the entire event. The dynamic nature of pop-up events made itself known as Julie submitted approximately eleven tag requests daily, responding to real-time sales patterns that dictated inventory replenishment throughout the day. The actual tag requirements dramatically exceeded initial projections, necessitating the on-site printing of approximately 10,000 additional tag (~200% variance from the original guestimate).
The volunteer composition presented additional operational constraints. 
The tent operation relied predominantly on part-time, teen volunteers with minimal adult supervision, resulting in inconsistent adherence to SOPs. This workforce limitation created a critical oversight gap, particularly in the hot foods service area where JWO oversite could not easily reach. The combination of volunteer staffing, limited JWO oversight, and dynamic inventory logistics fall into familiar pop-up patterns seen in previous events culminating in a need for more of an guiding JWO Ops presence than any actual technical guidance.
