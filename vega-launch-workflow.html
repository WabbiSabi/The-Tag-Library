RFID Tagging Workstream (New)
1. Tier classification determined (Tier 1/2/3) based on feasibility, venue logistics, merchant capacity
2. Service model selected (Model A / B / C) based on tier and merchant preference
3. Tagging quote generated and provided to merchant
4. Merchant approves → vendor engagement confirmed
5. Customer site(s) visited and logistics evaluated
6. Pre-print fulfillment initiated (Tier 2/3) OR on-site printing confirmed + consumables added to BOM
7. Vendor receives SOPs
